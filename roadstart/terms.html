3. Accuracy and official sources

  • We write every lesson and question in our own words, based on the official driver's manuals and the law, and we check them regularly.
  • Still, laws, fees and procedures change, and mistakes can happen.
  • The official driver's manual of your state and the law always come first. If the app says something different, follow the official source.
  • Each state's "About & sources" screen shows which manual edition the lessons were last checked against, with a link to it.
  • If you find a mistake, please tell us with "Report a mistake" in the app or at support@topjow.org. We fix mistakes for every user. A correction is never part of a paid feature.

4. Practice questions and test results

  • The practice questions are written by us. They are not the official test questions.

5. Translations

  • The app is written in English and translated into other languages.
  • We translate with care, and every translation is reviewed, but translations may contain mistakes.
  • If a translation and the English text differ, the English text applies. The official manual and the law apply in every case.
  • The real test may be given only in English, or in a few languages, depending on your state.

6. Safety
