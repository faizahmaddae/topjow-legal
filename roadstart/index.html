What Roadstart is

Roadstart teaches the driver's knowledge (permit) test for Maryland and Virginia in English, Persian (Farsi), Dari, Pashto, Arabic or Spanish: short lessons with pictures, road signs, practice questions with explanations, and practice tests in the format of the real test. It works without internet, has no account and no ads, and collects nothing.

Not a government app

Roadstart is an independent study app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the Maryland MVA, the Virginia DMV, or any government agency. Lessons are written in our own words from the official driver's manuals. Laws change — always check the official manual from your state before your test.

  • Maryland MVA, driver's manuals: mva.maryland.gov
  • Virginia DMV, driver's manual: dmv.virginia.gov

What is free, and the State Pass

Lessons, road signs and reviewing your mistakes are free. One practice test and one quick practice a day are free. Unlimited practice tests and chapter practice are an optional one-time purchase for each state (the State Pass). It is not a subscription.

The App Store or Google Play handles the payment and we never see it. Refunds are handled by Apple (reportaproblem.apple.com) or Google (your Google Play order history), not by us. On a new phone, use Settings → State Pass → Restore purchase with the same Apple or Google account.

My progress is gone

There is no account and no cloud copy: your lessons, answers and test results are stored only on your phone. Uninstalling the app, clearing its data or moving to a new phone leaves them behind, and we have no copy we could restore. A State Pass you bought can be restored (see above).
