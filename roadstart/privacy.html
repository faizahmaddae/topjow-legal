Information saved on your device

The app saves the following in its own storage on your device, so that it can work:

  • your chosen language, state and display settings;
  • your lesson progress, your answers, your practice test results, and the questions you saved;
  • the date of your real test, if you choose to enter it (the app uses it to suggest how many lessons to study each day);
  • whether you bought a State Pass, and how many free practice sessions you used today.

This information is not sent to us, and we cannot see it.

How to delete it:

  • Settings → "Reset my progress" deletes your progress, answers and test history.
  • Home → the menu next to your test date → "Remove date" deletes the date.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes everything the app saved.

If you turned on your phone's own backup (Apple iCloud backup or Google backup), that backup may include a copy of the app's data. Apple or Google stores it under their own privacy terms, and we have no access to it.

Purchases (State Pass)

The State Pass is an optional one-time purchase. Apple or Google processes it under their own terms and privacy policies:

  • Apple Media Services Terms and Conditions; Apple Privacy Policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
  • Google Play Terms of Service; Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

We never receive your name, email address, payment card or store account details. The app receives only a confirmation from the store that the State Pass was bought, and keeps it on your device. This lets it unlock the pass, including when you tap "Restore purchase".

On Android, Google Play's billing software is built into the app to make purchases possible. It may send technical information about the purchase process to Google, for example whether a purchase request succeeded or failed. Google uses this information under its own privacy policy, and we do not receive it.

Permissions

  • iPhone and iPad: the app asks for no permissions.
  • Android:
    • The app includes the billing permission that Google Play purchases need.
    • It also includes network access, which only Google Play's billing software uses.
    • The app's own features do not connect to the internet.

Telling a friend about the app

"Tell a friend about Roadstart" opens your phone's own share screen with a short message and a link to the app's page. You choose the app and the person. Nothing is sent unless you send it, and we do not see what you send or to whom.

Links to other websites

The app shows links to official government websites, such as the state's driver's manual. They open in your web browser. Those websites have their own privacy policies.

If you email us

"Report a mistake" opens your own email app with a message addressed to us. The draft already contains technical details that help us find the problem: the app and content version, your state and language, the question's code, and any recent app errors (short technical lines without personal information). You can read and change all of it before you send. We receive only what you choose to send, usually your email address and your message. We use it only to reply to you and to fix the app's content. We delete these emails after 12 months. To ask us to delete your email sooner, write to support@topjow.org.

Children

The app is made for people preparing for a learner's permit test. It is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children. The App Store or Google Play handles purchases by minors, including any parental approval they require.

Your choices and rights

We do not collect personal information, so we hold no information about you to access, correct or delete. Information saved on your device stays under your control (see "How to delete it" above). For any question, contact us.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this policy before the change takes effect and change the date above. If the change is significant, we will also tell you in the app.
