Privacy Policy

Roadstart: study app for the Maryland and Virginia driver's knowledge (permit) test · org.topjow.roadstart · for Android and iOS
Last updated: 20 November 2026

This policy explains how the Roadstart app ("the app") handles information. The app is published by Faiz Ahmad Dae ("we", "us"), an individual developer, not a company.

In short

  • The app works offline. It has no accounts and no ads, and it does not track you for advertising.
  • From version 1.2.0, the app sends anonymous usage data and crash reports, through Google's Firebase services, to help us find mistakes and improve the lessons. They never include your name, email address, phone number or advertising ID. You can turn this off in Settings (see "Usage data and crash reports" below).
  • We do not sell your information or use it for advertising. Google receives the usage data only to provide this service to us.
  • Your learning progress is saved on your device.
  • Purchases are made through the App Store (Apple) or Google Play (Google). We never see your payment details.

Information saved on your device

The app saves the following in its own storage on your device, so that it can work:

  • your chosen language, state and display settings;
  • your lesson progress, your answers, your practice test results, and the questions you saved;
  • the date of your real test, if you choose to enter it (the app uses it to suggest how many lessons to study each day);
  • whether you bought a State Pass, and how many free practice sessions you used today.

This saved information itself is not sent to us. But if "Share anonymous usage data" is on, the app sends a short note when some of these things happen, for example that a lesson was finished, a question was answered or saved, or a test date was set (only how many days are left). See "Usage data and crash reports" below.

How to delete it:

  • Settings → "Reset my progress" deletes your progress, answers and test history.
  • Home → the menu next to your test date → "Remove date" deletes the date.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes everything the app saved.

If you turned on your phone's own backup (Apple iCloud backup or Google backup), that backup may include a copy of the app's data. Apple or Google stores it under their own privacy terms, and we have no access to it.

Usage data and crash reports

From version 1.2.0, the app uses two Google services, Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics, to learn how the app is used and where it fails. Versions before 1.2.0 do not send usage data or crash reports.

What is sent:

  • What you do in the app: for example, which screens and lessons you open and finish, which practice questions you answer (by the question's code), which answer you chose and whether it was right, your practice test scores, which items you save, how many days are left until your test date (if you set one), the settings you change, how often and how long you use the app, whether the State Pass offer was shown, and whether a purchase went through (for a purchase, also the product, price and currency).
  • About the app and your phone: the app and content version, your chosen language and state, whether you own a State Pass, the phone model, the operating system version and its language.
  • Where the app came from: on Android, which store page or link the app was installed from.
  • Crash reports: when the app crashes or an error happens: technical details of the error, the last few things done in the app before it, the app and content version, your chosen language and state, whether you own a State Pass, the phone model and the operating system version.
  • Identifiers: random IDs that Firebase creates for this installation of the app. They are not connected to your name, your email or your store account. They usually change if you uninstall and reinstall the app.
  • Approximate location: Google estimates your country, region and city from your internet (IP) address. The app does not use your phone's location (GPS), and Google Analytics does not store your IP address.

What is never sent: your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, anything you type, your payment details, or your phone's advertising ID. The app does not ask for permission to track you, and it shows no ads.

Why: to find wrong or unclear questions and translations, to see where learners stop or have trouble, to fix crashes, and to improve the app and the State Pass offer. We do not use this data for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not combine it with information from other companies.

Who receives it: Google LLC processes this data for us as a service provider (Google works with it only for us), on servers that may be in the United States or other countries, under the Google Analytics and Firebase terms. We have not turned on Google's advertising features (such as "Google signals" or a link to Google Ads), and we have turned off sharing this data with other Google products. How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Firebase privacy information: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

How long it is kept: Google Analytics keeps detailed usage data for 14 months and then deletes it. Reports with only totals (for example, how many people finished a lesson) can be kept longer. Crash reports are deleted after 90 days. We may also keep a copy of the detailed usage data in our own Google Cloud storage, for at most 14 months.

How to turn it off: Settings → "Share anonymous usage data". It is on when you first install the app. When you turn it off, the app sends one last note that it was turned off. After that it sends no usage data and no crash reports, and crash reports not yet sent are deleted from your phone. You can turn it back on at any time.

Because this data does not include your name or contact details, we usually cannot find the data of one person. Turning the switch off stops new data. Uninstalling and reinstalling gives the app new random IDs, so new data is not connected to old data. Data already sent is deleted after the times above. To ask us to delete it sooner, write to support@topjow.org, and we will help as far as we can.

Purchases (State Pass)

The State Pass is an optional one-time purchase. Apple or Google processes it under their own terms and privacy policies:

  • Apple Media Services Terms and Conditions; Apple Privacy Policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
  • Google Play Terms of Service; Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

We never receive your name, email address, payment card or store account details. The app receives only a confirmation from the store that the State Pass was bought, and keeps it on your device. This lets it unlock the pass, including when you tap "Restore purchase".

On Android, Google Play's billing software is built into the app to make purchases possible. It may send technical information about the purchase process to Google, for example whether a purchase request succeeded or failed. Google uses this information under its own privacy policy, and we do not receive it.

Permissions

  • iPhone and iPad: the app asks for no permissions.
  • Android:
    • The app includes the billing permission that Google Play purchases need.
    • It also includes internet access, used by Google Play purchases and by the usage data and crash reports.
    • Firebase also needs two small permissions: one to send data in the background, and one to learn where the app was installed from.
    • The app does not include the advertising ID permission.
    • The app's learning features do not need the internet.

Telling a friend about the app

"Tell a friend about Roadstart" opens your phone's own share screen with a short message and a link to the app's page. You choose the app and the person. Nothing is sent unless you send it, and we do not see what you send or to whom.

Links to other websites

The app shows links to official government websites, such as the state's driver's manual. They open in your web browser. Those websites have their own privacy policies.

If you email us

"Report a mistake" opens your own email app with a message addressed to us. The draft already contains technical details that help us find the problem: the app and content version, your state and language, the question's code, and any recent app errors (short technical lines without personal information). You can read and change all of it before you send. If usage data is on, the app also notes that the report screen was opened (with the question's code), but never what you write. We receive only what you choose to send, usually your email address and your message. We use it only to reply to you and to fix the app's content. We delete these emails after 12 months. To ask us to delete your email sooner, write to support@topjow.org.

Children

The app is made for people preparing for a learner's permit test. It is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The anonymous usage data described above does not include a name, email or contact details. The App Store or Google Play handles purchases by minors, including any parental approval they require.

Your choices and rights

We do not collect your name, email or contact details through the app. The anonymous usage data and crash reports can be turned off at any time in Settings (see "Usage data and crash reports" above). Information saved on your device stays under your control (see "How to delete it" above). For any question or request, contact us.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this policy before the change takes effect and change the date above. If the change is significant, we will also tell you in the app.
