2. What stays on your device

Everything the app needs is kept in its private storage on your phone, which other apps cannot read:

  • your income and expense entries: amount, description, category, date and time;
  • your categories;
  • your settings: language, calendar, currency, digits, text size and theme;
  • a few small counters the app needs to behave as described here: how many free PDF reports you have used, when a backup was last suggested, and when the rating window (section 5) was last asked for.

Updating from the old version. On the first start after the update, the app reads the old version's database on the phone itself and copies your entries into the new one. Nothing is uploaded for this. The old file is left untouched on the phone.

Android Auto Backup. If backup to your Google account is turned on in Android, Android may include the app's data in that backup so it can be restored on a new phone. Google performs this backup under its own terms; the developer has no access to it.

3. Files you create

A backup file, a PDF report or an Excel (CSV) file is created only when you ask for one. It is handed to Android's share screen, and you choose where it goes (for example WhatsApp, Telegram, Google Drive or a printer). From there, the app you chose handles it under its own privacy policy. The developer never receives these files.

When you restore a backup, Android's file picker lets you choose one file; the app reads only that file, on the phone.

4. Ads (Google AdMob) and your consent

The Android app is free and carries ads from Google AdMob. Where and when they can appear is part of the product:

  • a banner above the navigation bar, with a gap so that a tap meant for a tab does not land on it;
  • an ad when you open the app, shown on the start screen before the app appears, and when you come back to it — never at the very first start, at least four minutes apart, and not when you return from sharing, printing, picking a file, writing an email or the store page;
  • an occasional full-screen ad after an export (PDF or Excel) has finished — never while you are entering money, at least 30 seconds apart and with at least two actions in between;
  • after four free PDF reports, the app offers a short rewarded video before each further PDF report. You decide; if no ad can be shown, the report is made anyway. Excel files and backups are never behind an ad;
  • if you activated the old version's paid “pro” licence, the app shows no ads at all.

To serve and measure an ad, Google processes among other things the device's advertising identifier (Android's advertising ID), the IP address, device type and operating system, an approximate location derived from the IP address, and your interaction with the ad. Google publishes the details and the list of advertising partners itself: business.safety.google/privacy and Google's GDPR notes for AdMob. Your income and expense entries are never given to Google or to anyone else.

Consent. In the EU/EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google's User Messaging Platform checks your consent status and shows its form where one is required. No ad is requested before that status is resolved.

Changing your mind. Where consent applies, Settings → “Ad privacy settings” reopens the same form. The row appears only where Google says it leads somewhere; outside those regions it is not shown.

Legal basis: your consent, Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and § 25(1) TDDDG, for storing and reading information on your device for advertising. Withdrawal takes effect for the future.

Recipient: Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland, as the provider of the advertising service in its own right.

The internet permission the app declares is used only by the advertising SDK. The app itself works fully offline. A build that carries no ad unit (such as the iOS build) does not start the advertising SDK at all.

5. Rating the app

After you have used the app for a while, it may occasionally ask Google Play to show its rating window. Google Play decides whether it appears; any rating or review you write goes directly to Google Play under Google's privacy policy. The app never learns what you wrote, or whether you rated it at all.

6. What the app does not do

  • No account, no registration, no email address, no password.
  • No access to location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos: the app asks for none of those permissions. (The approximate location in section 4 is Google's estimate from the IP address.)
  • No notifications and no messages from us.
  • No server of our own, and no analytics or crash reporting of our own.
  • No selling of data, and no sharing beyond the services named on this page.

9. Your rights, and how to use them

Under the GDPR you have the right of access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20), and to object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21). Consent you have given can be withdrawn at any time with effect for the future (Art. 7(3)).

In practice the developer holds nothing about you: your entries exist only on your phone. What you can do:

  • Take your data with you: make a backup, or export a PDF or Excel file.
  • Delete everything: delete entries in the app, or uninstall it.
  • Change or withdraw ad consent: Settings → “Ad privacy settings”, where consent applies.
  • Reset your advertising identifier or turn off personalised ads for the whole device: in Android's settings.

For any request write to support@topjow.org. Please say what you need; we answer within the one-month period of Art. 12(3) GDPR.

You also have the right to complain to a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR) — either where you live or the one responsible for the controller.

10. Children

Masaref is a money diary for a general adult audience and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect data from children, and it asks for no personal information from anyone. If you believe a child has provided personal data through this app, write to support@topjow.org.
