Support

App «مصارف» (Masaref) — simple income and expense tracker · for Android and iPhone
Last updated: 19 November 2026

Contact us

  • Email support@topjow.org. You can write in English, Dari or Persian.
  • Please include your phone model, its Android or iOS version, and the app's version, which is at the bottom of Settings.

How do I add money I got or spent?

On the home screen, press the green Income button for money you received, or the red Expense button for money you spent. Type the amount, choose a category if you like, and press Save. The total balance and this month's income and expenses update straight away.

How do I change or delete an entry?

Open the List tab and tap an entry to change it. To delete one, swipe it sideways; Undo appears for a few seconds if you change your mind.

Backups and moving to a new phone

There is no account and no cloud copy of our own: your entries are stored only on your phone. To keep them safe, open Settings → Backup & restore, press Make a backup, then send it to yourself (for example on WhatsApp, Telegram, Google Drive or Files). On the new phone, install Masaref and choose Restore from a file with that file. Restoring never creates duplicates. Uninstalling the app without a backup deletes your entries, and we have no copy we could restore.

PDF and Excel reports

The Reports tab shows charts by category, day and month. From there you can make a PDF report to print or send, or an Excel (CSV) file. The first four PDF reports are free; after that the app offers a short video ad before each further PDF report. Excel files and backups are always free.

Language, calendar, currency and numbers

Change them at any time in Settings: Dari, Persian or English; the Solar Hijri calendar with Afghan month names (Hamal, Sawr, …), with Iranian month names, or the Gregorian calendar; Afghani, Toman, Rial, Dollar, Euro, Rupee or any other currency; and Dari or English digits. You can also make the text larger and turn on the dark theme.

Ads and privacy settings

  • The app is free and shows Google AdMob ads. Your income and expense entries are never sent to us, to Google or to anyone else.
  • Where it applies, Settings → Ad privacy settings reopens Google's consent choices. On iPhone, tracking permission is in the device's Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes everything stored on the phone.

The details are in the privacy policy.
