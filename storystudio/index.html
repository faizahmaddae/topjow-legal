Privacy Policy — Story Studio

Effective date: 20 October 2026

In short

  • Story Studio is a photo editor: you put text, stickers and shapes on your photos and make posts and stories. It has no accounts and no sign-in, and we run no server.
  • Your photos never leave your phone. The photos you open, the designs you make and the subject the app cuts out of a photo are all processed on the device. Nothing you edit is uploaded, to us or to anyone else.
  • Three Google services receive data from the app: Google AdMob (the ads that keep it free), Google Analytics for Firebase (which features people use) and Firebase Crashlytics (crash reports).
  • We never ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts or precise location, and we do not sell personal information.
  • Where the law asks for it, the app asks for your choices about ads, and you can change them at any time in Settings → Ad privacy.

Who we are

Story Studio (“the app”) is published by Faiz Ahmad Dae, trading as WebJow, the developer named on its Google Play page (“we”, “us”), who is responsible for this policy. You can reach us at support@topjow.org.

Your photos and designs

  • Opening a photo. When you pick a photo, the app opens only that photo, through the system's photo picker. It does not browse your library.
  • Taking a photo. When you choose the camera, the app opens your phone's own camera app; the photo taken goes into your design only, not into your gallery. The app itself asks for no camera permission.
  • Cut out and blur background. Finding the subject of a photo is done on the phone by the system's on-device image analysis. The photo is not sent anywhere for it.
  • Your designs are saved in the app's own storage on your phone so you can open them again. We cannot see them; deleting the app deletes them.
  • Saving and sharing. When you export a design, it is saved to your gallery or handed to your phone's share sheet, and goes only where you send it.

What else the app keeps on your phone

Your settings (language, appearance) and whether you have seen the introduction. Deleting the app deletes them.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads from Google AdMob: banners and native ads in some screens, an occasional full-screen ad, and a video you can choose to watch to save a design in higher quality. To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google collects from the app:

  • device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID (unless you have deleted it) and other identifiers of the device or app install;
  • your approximate location, worked out from your IP address;
  • information about the ads you see and how you interact with them and with the app;
  • diagnostic and performance information about the ads.

Google receives this data directly from the app, under its own privacy policy and its ads data protection terms; we do not receive a copy of it. Google explains how it uses it in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Your choices about ads. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and other regions whose law asks for it, the app shows Google's privacy form before ads are personalised, and they are personalised only if you agree. You can change your answer at any time in Settings → Ad privacy (shown wherever your region asks for these choices). On Android you can also reset or delete your advertising ID in the phone's settings (Privacy → Ads, or Google → Ads).

Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

To learn which features are used, the app records a small set of events: the screens you open, that the introduction was finished (and in which language), that a design was opened (blank, from a photo or from a template, and which template), that a design was exported (shared or saved, its format and size) and that a higher-quality export was unlocked. Events carry no text you typed, no photos and no names.

Google Analytics also records the device model, the operating system and app version, the language, an approximate location worked out from your IP address, and a random identifier for this install of the app. The app starts measuring only after the privacy choices above have been answered, or where your region does not ask for them. Where your region asks for consent, what Analytics may store and whether its data may be used for ads follow your answer.

Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

If the app crashes or meets an error, Firebase Crashlytics sends a report so we can fix it: the technical trace of the error, the device model, the operating system and app version, and a random identifier for this install. Crash reports are not linked to you and are never used for advertising.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, no sign-in, no server of ours.
  • No uploading of your photos or designs, and no reading of photos you did not pick.
  • No access to your contacts, microphone or precise location.
  • No selling of personal information, and no data brokers. Personalised ads through AdMob may count as “sharing” or “targeted advertising” under some US state laws; you can opt out as described under “Your choices about ads”.

Children

Story Studio is a general-audience app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children; if you believe a child has used the app and want something removed, write to us.

Legal bases (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

  • Consent: personalised ads, and analytics where your region asks for consent.
  • Legitimate interests: crash reports (to keep the app working), non-personalised ads, their measurement and fraud prevention (to keep the app free).

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data, and to complain to your data protection authority. Because we hold no account and receive no copy of the ads data, most of your data lives on your phone and with Google: you can reset your advertising ID, change your ad choices in the app, and use Google's own privacy controls. For anything else, write to support@topjow.org and we will answer within 30 days.

Changes

If we change what the app collects, we will update this page and its effective date before the new version reaches you.
