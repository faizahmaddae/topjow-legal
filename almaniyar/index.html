2. In short

Almaniyar teaches German to Persian and Dari speakers. It has no account, no login and no server of its own. Everything you learn — your lessons, your streak, your review cards — is written to a file on your phone and stays there. Nobody, including us, can read it.

Three things can leave your phone, and each one is either off until you turn it on or handled entirely by Google or Apple:

  1. Usage statistics and crash reports. Off by default. They only start if you switch them on.
  2. Advertising. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Before the first ad, Google's consent form asks you what is allowed.
  3. The subscription. If you buy Almaniyar Plus, the purchase happens inside Google Play or the App Store. We never see your payment details.

Your voice from the speaking exercises is a fourth case, and section 4.4 explains it honestly.

3. What stays on your device

The app writes one file, with a backup copy, in the app-support folder that the operating system gives Almaniyar. On iOS this folder is not exposed to file sharing. If the file is ever damaged the app keeps the broken copy so your progress can be recovered by hand.

What it stores is:

  • Your settings — interface language, Dari wording on or off, light/dark theme, the colour-vision variant of the der/die/das colours, how much of the app animates, haptics, sound effects, the speech rate, your daily goal.
  • Your course — which lessons you finished, your crowns per unit, the unit a placement test opened for you, your total XP, your streak and freezes, your hearts and when they refill, your review cards with their box and due date, which day you last played the daily article challenge, your best game scores, and the last lesson you opened.
  • A few flags — whether onboarding is done, whether the placement test was taken or declined, whether you accepted the daily reminder and at what hour, whether you allowed usage statistics, whether the ad consent flow has run, whether Plus was active the last time the store answered, how many lessons have passed since the last ad, and whether the rating prompt has been shown.

The advertising component keeps its own small counters on the device — the times at which ads were shown, so the frequency limits work. They are never sent anywhere.

None of this leaves the phone. There is no upload, no sync, no backup to us, and no way for us to see it. It is deleted when you delete the app.

The course itself — every lesson, every word, every grammar tip — ships inside the app as a bundled file. The app downloads no content, so it works with no network at all.

4. What leaves your device

4.1 Usage statistics and crash reports (Firebase)

Default: off. The stored setting starts as “off”, and the app applies that stored value to the Firebase SDKs at every launch before sending anything.

On first launch the app asks “Help make Almaniyar better?”. Only “Yes, send anonymous reports” turns collection on; “No, send nothing” leaves it off. You can change your answer at any time under Settings → Privacy → “Usage statistics and crash reports”. If you never consent, nothing is collected.

If you consent, Almaniyar sends Google Firebase Analytics short events about how the app is used. This is the complete list:

Plus a screen name each time you move to a new screen, and four per-install properties: interface language, Dari wording, daily goal, Plus active.

Three rules are enforced in the code, not just promised here:

  • No user id is ever set. The app never calls setUserId.
  • Nothing you type is sent. Every parameter is a number, an enum name or a boolean; anything containing a space or a slash is replaced by <redacted> before it is sent. A German sentence you typed, a transcript of your voice and a file path cannot get through.
  • Screen names, not routes. The screen reporter drops anything that looks like a path, so /lesson/u03_l02 — which would say what you are working through — is never sent.

Firebase Analytics itself also collects, on Google's side, technical data we do not choose: an app-instance identifier, device model, operating system version and country. No advertising identifier is read for Analytics, and Google Analytics' ad-related consent signals are set to “denied” by default in the app: the statistics are for improving the app, not for advertising. Google's own description is at firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Crash reports go to Firebase Crashlytics under the same consent. A crash report contains the error, the stack trace, the device model, the OS version, and the last few navigation breadcrumbs — screen names, never content. Errors the app recovers from are also filed, capped so one repeated fault cannot flood.

Legal basis: your consent, Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, and § 25(1) TDDDG for the information stored on and read from your device. You can withdraw it at any time — see section 10.

Processor: Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland.

The crash reporter is installed very early in start-up — before the stored preference is applied — on purpose, because otherwise a failure during start-up would not be captured at all. So that this window catches nobody who never consented, collection is off at build time, on Android and on iOS. Someone who did consent overrides that on the next launch through the Firebase SDK's own persisted setting.

And the switch takes effect immediately, both ways: turn it off and from that second nothing more is handed over — no event, no crash report, no navigation step — and whatever the two SDKs had already buffered is discarded, so yesterday's report is not uploaded tomorrow after all.

4.2 Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of Almaniyar shows ads. The paid Plus subscription removes them completely.

There are three places an ad can appear, and no others:

  • a banner at the bottom of the app's main sections, above the tab bar;
  • a full-screen ad after every second finished lesson, at most three per hour and never less than four minutes apart;
  • a rewarded video you choose to watch, to refill hearts or double a lesson's XP — never forced, and the reward is granted from the ad SDK's own reward callback, so you always get what you were promised.

There is no ad when the app is opened (no app-open ad).

Never inside a lesson, never inside a review session and never inside the article game. That is a product rule the developer set, and it is enforced by where the banner is allowed to be mounted.

During the first-launch steps the advertising component stays off; it starts only once you have arrived on the learning path. Before the first ad, Google's User Messaging Platform shows you a consent form. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland it asks whether Google and its ad partners may use your data for personalised advertising. Almaniyar shows a short explanation in the app's language first, then Google's real form; the explanation cannot answer for you.

If you consent, Google and the ad partners you agreed to may read and use your device's advertising identifier, approximate location derived from your IP address, and information about the ads you saw and tapped, to choose and measure ads. If you refuse, you still see ads, but non-personalised ones — which still involve reading and writing an identifier on your device for frequency capping and fraud prevention.

Apple ATT (iOS): on iOS, Apple's own App Tracking Transparency prompt comes first, again with a short explanation from the app in front of it. Saying no there means the advertising identifier is not available to anyone, whatever you answer afterwards.

Almaniyar itself never receives your advertising identifier, never receives what ad you saw, and never joins any of it to your progress. What the app keeps is a count of how long ago the last ad was, so the limits above work.

Legal basis: your consent, Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and § 25(1) TDDDG, collected through Google's consent form. You can reopen that form at any time — see section 10.

Recipient: Google Ireland Limited (address as above), as the provider of the advertising services in its own right.

Google's description of what it does with ad data: business.safety.google/privacy and Google's GDPR notes for AdMob.

4.3 The Plus subscription

Almaniyar Plus is an auto-renewing subscription, monthly or yearly, with a seven-day free trial once.

The purchase itself happens entirely inside Google Play or the Apple App Store. Almaniyar never sees, asks for or stores your name, your email address, your card or your billing address. What the app receives back from the store is which product was bought and whether it is currently active. It writes a single true/false to the device so the first screen after launch does not show ads to a subscriber while the store is still answering.

Almaniyar does not pass any identifier of yours to the store — the purchase is not tagged with an account name of ours, because there is no account.

For the payment itself, Google and Apple are the responsible parties, not us. Their terms: Google · Apple.

Legal basis for handling the entitlement flag: performance of the contract you entered into for Plus, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

4.4 The microphone and speech recognition

The speaking exercises ask you to say a German sentence and score how close you came.

Almaniyar never records your voice to a file, never stores it and never uploads it. What the app receives is text — the words the recogniser thinks it heard — and that text is scored on the device and then discarded. It is never written to storage and never sent to analytics.

The recognition is not done by Almaniyar. It is done by the speech recogniser built into your phone, which is a separate part of the operating system or a separate app. Almaniyar asks it, on every single session, to work on the device:

  • On iOS this sets requiresOnDeviceRecognition, which Apple enforces. If the German model is not installed, the attempt fails and Almaniyar offers you the “repeat after me” exercise instead. Your voice does not go to Apple.
  • On Android this sets EXTRA_PREFER_OFFLINE on the request to whichever recogniser your phone has. Google's recogniser honours it when the German offline pack is installed. If recognition then fails with a network error, Almaniyar reads that as “the offline pack is missing” and stops, rather than trying again online.

The honest caveat: on Android that setting is a request to a component we do not control. A manufacturer's or third-party recogniser could ignore it and send the audio to its own servers for processing, and Almaniyar has no way to see that from the outside. If that is not acceptable to you, do not grant the microphone permission. Every speaking exercise has a “repeat after me” alternative that counts exactly the same, and no lesson is ever blocked by a refused microphone.

Almaniyar asks for the microphone only when you tap the microphone button for the first time, and explains why before the system dialog appears.

Legal basis: your consent, Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, given by granting the permission, withdrawable in your phone's settings at any time. For the on-device processing, we are not a recipient of any data at all.

4.5 Spoken German (text to speech)

The app reads German sentences aloud using the voice engine built into your phone. The text sent to that engine is course material — German sentences that ship inside the app — never anything you typed or said. Some voice engines download voice data the first time they speak a language; that is between the engine and its maker, and it contains nothing about you.

4.6 Daily reminder

If you turn it on, Almaniyar schedules one daily notification at the hour you choose. It is a local notification: it is created and fired by your own phone. There is no push service, no device token, and nothing is sent anywhere. Turning it off removes it.

Legal basis: your consent, Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, withdrawable in the app or in your phone's settings.

4.7 Rating and links

The “rate Almaniyar” row asks Google Play or the App Store to show their own rating sheet; the store decides whether to show anything, and never tells us. Links in the app — this policy, credits — open in your browser. Opening a link means the site you land on sees the request, as with any link.

No text you typed, sent anywhere. No advertising identifier read by us. No profile that follows you between apps or devices, and nothing sold to anyone — ever.

9. How long anything is kept

  • On your phone: until you delete the app. Deleting the app deletes the file and everything in it.
  • Firebase Analytics: 2 months for user-level and event-level data (the retention period set for this app); standard aggregated, non-identifying reports may be kept longer.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: Google deletes crash reports after 90 days.
  • AdMob: according to Google's own retention rules, which we do not set.
  • Google Play / App Store purchases: according to Google's and Apple's rules; we hold nothing about the purchase except a true/false on your device.

10. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and objection (Art. 21). You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR) — either where you live or the authority responsible for the controller.

How to actually exercise them, given that there is no account:

  • Withdraw consent to statistics and crash reports: Settings → Privacy → “Usage statistics and crash reports”. The switch takes effect immediately and for the future.
  • Change your advertising consent: the “Ad settings” entry in the Privacy section of Almaniyar's settings reopens Google's consent form, where you can change or withdraw what you allowed. The entry is shown wherever Google's form applies to you. On iOS you can also change tracking under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Withdraw the microphone or notification permission: your phone's system settings, at any time.
  • Erase everything on the device: delete the app.
  • Anything else: write to Support@topjow.org (see section 1).

One honest limitation, under Art. 11 GDPR: because Almaniyar has no account and sets no user id, we cannot connect a request to a particular person's data on Google's side. If you write to us we will tell you exactly what exists and how to reach Google's own controls, but we cannot look up “your” record, because there isn't one keyed to you.

11. Children

Almaniyar is rated 4+ on the App Store and Everyone on Google Play, because nothing in it is unsuitable for a young reader. That rating is not the same as being made for children.

Almaniyar is not directed to children. It is a course for adults and teenagers learning German — people arriving in a German-speaking country, in an integration course, or preparing for work. Almaniyar is not enrolled in Google Play's Families programme and is not in the App Store's Kids Category.

What that means in practice:

  • We do not knowingly collect data from children, and we cannot, because we collect no personal data at all without consent and no identifying data even with it.
  • Ads are served as for a general audience, and only ads rated for all ages (Google’s “G” rating) are shown.
  • In Germany a person under 16 cannot validly consent alone (Art. 8 GDPR); a parent or guardian must decide. If a child in your care has switched the statistics on or accepted personalised ads, switch it back in the app's settings and write to us.
