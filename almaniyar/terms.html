1. What the app is

Almaniyar is an independent course that teaches German at level A1 to speakers of Persian and Dari. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to the Goethe-Institut, telc, the German Federal Office for Migration and Refugees (BAMF), any integration-course provider or any government agency. The app does not award certificates and is not an exam.

2. The learning content

We write the lessons, explanations and exercises ourselves and check them, but mistakes can happen. If you find one, please tell us at support@topjow.org.

Some lessons teach the language of offices, doctors, pharmacies and emergencies. They teach language, not legal or medical advice. In an emergency in Germany, call 112.

3. The free version and ads

The whole course is free to use. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner on the main screens, and occasionally a full-screen ad between lessons. No ad appears inside a lesson or inside the article game. How ads use your data, and the choices you have, are explained in the Privacy Policy.

Some features let you choose to watch an ad for an in-app reward — for example to refill hearts or to double the points of a finished lesson. Watching is always optional, and the reward is given only inside the app.

4. Hearts, points and streaks

Hearts, points (XP), levels and streaks are parts of the game inside the app. They have no money value, cannot be bought, sold, exchanged or transferred, and may change as the app changes. Hearts refill over time, by an optional ad, or without limit with Plus. Review and the article game never cost hearts.
